# The Orchard Between Worlds

An original, fictional interactive myth inspired by Persephone, Hecate, the Shadow, and individuation.

## Structure

- `assets/`: five coordinated, AI-generated WebP chapter illustrations (fictional characters).
- The interactive site is deployed to the Cloudflare Worker `graves-persephone-story` at `https://graves.ptelectronics.net/persephone/`.

The illustrations are symbolic fiction, not portraits of actual people. No private communications or sensitive biographical records are published. Visitors' journal entries and progress stay in their own browser.

The creative experience belongs to its participants; it is not a diagnosis or behavioral assessment. Public use of identifiable private material requires specific approval.
